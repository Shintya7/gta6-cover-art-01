Vice Cover Studio — a GTA 6-style cover art maker that turns your photos and a caption into a multi-panel poster with a sunset or neon colour grade, and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Pick a layout (Poster, Triptych or Single), fill each panel with your own photo or a sample, drag to frame it, grade it, letter the caption in bold Anton type — then export in one click. Runs fully offline in the browser; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
